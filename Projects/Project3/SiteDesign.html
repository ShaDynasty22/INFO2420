Native Cooking of Utah: Site Design

A welcoming palette for home cooks, inspired by the supplied food photographs: cream, dark brown, leafy green, and raspberry.

Color Palette

  • Background: #FFF8EF
  • Text: #382D26 (contrast 12.68:1)
  • Headings: #713743 (contrast 8.55:1)
  • Subheadings: #3D542D (contrast 7.96:1)
  • Links: #31562E (contrast 7.97:1)
  • Visited links: #70446C (contrast 7.29:1)
  • Hovered links: #8A391C (contrast 7.43:1)

Typography and Images

Arial body text at 100%, Georgia headings at 200%, 150%, and 120%, and italic quotations. Photographs retain their original dimensions and float left or right with 10px of padding between the image and text.

Contrast ratios above are calculated against the cream background using relative luminance. This reference page was created directly; it is not a Paletton export.
